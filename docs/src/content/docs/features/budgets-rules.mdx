## Budgets

Budgets are per-category limits — week, month, or year — with current-period progress shown on the Budget page, and your spending against the budget line in Trends. Transfer categories are excluded from budgets by default; see [Categories & cashflow](/features/categories-cashflow/).

## Rules

Categorization rules match transactions and apply a category and/or tags. Rules run automatically on new imports from bank sync, and can be applied to existing transactions.

:::note
Budgets and rules are per account — each user sees and edits their own.
:::
