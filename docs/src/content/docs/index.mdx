**Calm seas. Clear books.**

Galene is a self-hostable personal finance app. It tracks transactions across your accounts, categorizes them, and shows you where your money goes — on your own host.

## What it does

- **Accounts and transactions** — import from a bank (SimpleFIN, Plaid, or a built-in demo bank) or enter them by hand; [import and export CSV](/features/import-transactions/) on the same columns; categorize (including transfer categories for credit card payments), tag, search, and filter, including empty fields.
- **Budgets and rules** — per-category limits with progress, and categorization rules that run on new imports. On Trends, the budget line and a bar drill into the transactions behind them.
- **Planning** — scheduled expectations, a cashflow forecast, and spending trends against your budget.
- **Advisor access** — a frozen [Accountant Pack](/features/advisor-access/) or a read-only Viewer for a date range, without another Galene login.
- **API and MCP** — a read-only REST API and a bundled MCP server (stdio, or HTTP with an API token), so scripts and assistants can read your data.

Everything runs on a single SQLite file in a single container. There is no cloud, no vendor account, and no service between you and your data.

## How these docs are organized

- **Start** — get a running instance, get an account, manage API tokens, and understand the configuration.
- **Self-host** — Docker & Compose, Podman (compose & quadlets), from source, reverse proxy, and backups.
- **Features** — what the app does, one area at a time: bank sync (SimpleFIN, Plaid), budgets & rules, categories & cashflow, accounts & tags, import and export, [advisor access](/features/advisor-access/), and the [API & MCP](/features/api-mcp/).
- **Reference** — environment variables, in one place.
