---
title: Bank sync
description: Import accounts and transactions from SimpleFIN, Plaid, or the demo bank.
---

Galene imports accounts and transactions from bank providers through a common sync interface (Settings → Bank sync). Three providers ship:

- **Demo Bank** — built-in mock with demo accounts and ~3 months of realistic transactions; makes no external calls.
- **SimpleFIN** — the SimpleFIN protocol (SimpleFIN's bridge, or any bank that implements it): you paste a one-time setup token, and Galene claims it once for a long-lived access URL. Setup: [SimpleFIN](/features/simplefin/).
- **Plaid** — live bank accounts through Plaid Link: you pick your bank and log in in Plaid's hosted widget. Setup: [Plaid](/features/plaid/).

Syncs are incremental: each pull starts at the newest transaction date already imported, and imports are deduplicated by provider + external id. When Plaid posts a charge and names the pending id, Galene keeps the posted amount, date, and id, copies category, notes, color, tags, and splits only onto empty posted fields, and deletes the pending row. It does not import that pending row again when the same response already posts it. Two charges are not merged on merchant and amount alone. A pending id that disappeared from the feed still folds into one matching posted row. Your categorization rules run on new imports. Disconnecting a connection keeps the accounts and transactions already imported.

## Auto-sync

Each connection can auto-sync on a schedule. The server checks every minute and syncs connections whose next sync time has passed; a failed sync backs off to at least an hour. Intervals are bounded per provider:

| Provider | Minimum interval | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo Bank | 15 minutes | no external API to rate-limit |
| SimpleFIN | 2 hours | the bridge allows 24 requests/day per access, and one sync is one request |
| Plaid | 15 minutes | Plaid rate-limits per Item (15 req/min `/accounts/get`, 30 req/min `/transactions/get`); a sync makes a few calls per linked bank |

## Provider (bank) balance

On each successful SimpleFIN or Plaid sync, Galene stores the institution-reported **bank balance** (signed cents) and an **as-of** timestamp per linked account — SimpleFIN’s `balance` / `balance-date` when present, otherwise Plaid’s current/available balance stamped at fetch time. Demo Bank also reports sample balances.

The Home **Balances** widget and **Settings → Accounts** show **Ledger** (Galene’s computed balance, including any opening-balance anchor) beside **Bank** when a provider balance exists. Manual accounts never show a Bank value. Galene does **not** auto-adjust the ledger to match the bank. On **Settings → Accounts → Edit**, you can apply a **suggested opening** from bank − transactions (user confirm only) — see [Accounts & tags](/features/accounts-tags/).
